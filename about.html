Our Story

The Beginning

One bride in the salon at a time

Founded in 2015, Atelier was born from a desire to bring the intimacy and precision of French couture to the modern bride. The journey of finding a gown matters as much as the day it is worn.

Our salon is a sanctuary. Brides explore the collections in private, guided by a stylist who understands the nuances of fabric, fit and form — and who will tell you honestly when a gown is not right.

Every gown we house is selected for its craftsmanship. Our in-house master tailors sculpt each piece to your silhouette, which is the only way a dress ever truly becomes yours.
