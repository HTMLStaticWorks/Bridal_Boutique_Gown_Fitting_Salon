The Experience

Your vision becomes our obsession

From the moment you step into the salon, one stylist is yours — through the first rail, the long pause in front of the mirror, and every fitting after.

Most brides find their gown in the second hour. Some find it in the first ten minutes and spend the rest of the afternoon making sure. Both are welcome here.

The Process

From first rail to final press

Three appointments across roughly eight months. We hold your hand through all of them.

Consultation

Two private hours. We listen first, pull gowns second, and never bring out more than you asked for.

Selection

Your gown is ordered to your measurements from the atelier that made the sample.

Alterations
