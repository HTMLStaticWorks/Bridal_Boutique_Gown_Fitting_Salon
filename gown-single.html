The Genevieve

$2,400 Excluding alterations · Estimate at fitting

The Genevieve embodies timeless romance. Cut from luxurious silk Mikado, this A-line gown pairs a softly draped bodice with an off-the-shoulder neckline and a dramatic chapel-length train — classic sophistication with one modern turn.

Mikado holds its shape without boning, so the gown sits clean through the waist and moves as one piece. It is the gown we reach for when a bride says she wants to be comfortable and still be seen.
